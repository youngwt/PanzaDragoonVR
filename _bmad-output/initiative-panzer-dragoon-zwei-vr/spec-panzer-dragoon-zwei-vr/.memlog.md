- (event) create, express mode: input rich (brainstorm milestone-roadmap.md, research-notes.md, brainstorm memlog); no elicitation
- (decision) slug panzer-dragoon-zwei-vr inherited from the brainstorm folder; initiative-panzer-dragoon-zwei-vr created to hold it
- (direction) Why: a vision to realize - passion project and the user's first VR project; ride the dragon through the Episode 3 forest with a full 360-degree view. Target: playable demo within a year of 2026-10-06, shared on SideQuest bring-your-own-ROM
- (direction) Two tracks: pipeline (PC extraction tool) and flight (Godot game on headset); they meet only at the external asset folder plus manifest
- (capability) CAP-1 VR scene on Quest 3: player can look all around a 3D scene in the headset. Success: a C# Godot APK runs on Quest 3 at a steady frame rate showing a ground plane, a sky and a grey cube flying around the player (milestone 0)
- (capability) CAP-2 external assets: the game ships with no assets, loads everything at runtime from a known folder on the headset and checks it against a manifest at launch. Success: ground and sky in the CAP-1 scene are textured from the user's own example images copied into that folder; a file the manifest lists but the folder lacks is reported at launch (milestone 0)
- (capability) CAP-3 extraction tool: a user runs a PC tool on their own Zwei disc image and gets the demo's assets as ordinary files plus the manifest. Success: one run writes the Episode 3 images and they are recognisable by eye in a file explorer (milestone 1)
- (capability) CAP-4 forest floor and canopy: player flies through the Episode 3 forest between the extracted floor and canopy. Success: in the headset the floor loops endlessly beneath the player and the canopy sits overhead with no parallax (milestone 2)
- (capability) CAP-5 riding the dragon: player sits mid-way along the extracted dragon's back. Success: in the headset the wings are either side, the head in front and the tail behind (milestone 3)
- (capability) CAP-6 reticle: player aims a reticle by pointing the aiming-hand controller. Success: the reticle follows the hand at a fixed distance around the player and changes colour over a target (milestone 4)
- (capability) CAP-7 gun in hand: player sees a gun where the aiming hand is. Success: a gun model tracks the controller; the Quest controller model satisfies it, a bone-like Panzer Dragoon gun is the later form (milestone 5)
- (capability) CAP-8 shooting: player destroys targets with rapid shots or lock-on lasers. Success: grey cubes corkscrew around the player; top trigger fires rapid shots; holding a face button locks up to four targets, each marked by a cloned reticle; releasing fires homing lasers that destroy them (milestone 6)
- (capability) CAP-9 real enemies: the targets are the Episode 3 flying enemies extracted from the disc. Success: the CAP-8 lock-on volley destroys four extracted Zwei enemies in the headset (milestone 7)
- (constraint) Nothing copyrighted in git or in the APK: disc image, extracted or decoded assets, BIOS, soundtrack
- (constraint) All Saturn-format knowledge lives in the PC extraction tool; the game reads only standard formats (PNG and similar)
- (constraint) The tracks share only the external asset folder and manifest; flight work runs on own example images and grey cubes so it never waits on extraction
- (constraint) Extraction runs on the PC, once; never on the headset
- (constraint) Platform: standalone Quest 3, Godot 4.7, C# from the first line (C# on Android is labelled experimental; milestone 0 exists to test it)
- (constraint) Known folder is the app's own Android/data/<package>/files: Quest scoped storage blocks arbitrary /sdcard paths and MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE is not available to this kind of app
- (constraint) On rails as in the original: the dragon always moves forward
- (constraint) No full-body IK; hands are floating controllers or guns
- (constraint) Information lives in the world, not on a HUD; the radar is the one permitted HUD element
- (constraint) Original assets are used as extracted, however rough they look in VR; no upscaled or redrawn art
- (constraint) Plane distances (floor depth, canopy height) are tuning values set in the headset, not fixed up front
- (constraint) Every milestone ends in something recordable in fifteen seconds; no dates are set per milestone
- (decision) Extracted images are checked by eye; no formal reference comparison
- (decision) Rez Infinite is the reference for reticle and aiming feel
- (decision) Milestones 4, 5 and 6 need nothing from the disc and can be built while extraction is in progress
- (decision) Non-goals for this demo: episodes other than 3; everything in later-ideas.md (health, berserk, steering, view turns, radar build, sound, real flight paths, centipedes, boss, bundled release); a custom asset viewer; dates per milestone
- (direction) Success signal: within a year of 2026-10-06 a player who has run the extractor on their own disc rides the dragon through the Episode 3 forest on a Quest 3 and destroys four real Zwei enemies with one lock-on volley, with no copyrighted content in the repo or APK
